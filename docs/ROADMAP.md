# Roadmap

This file is the plan of market. The Status page of the site reads it, and the tracker of the project mirrors it.

| ID | Item | Status | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | The hand crawl | done, 2026-11-12 | The record is [`HAND-CRAWL.md`](HAND-CRAWL.md): 22 sellers and aggregators, their official APIs, their `robots.txt` files, their terms and the fields of an offer. The terms of each official API limit how long a program keeps the data. Thus a stored history (R5) needs the consent of each seller, or a source that sells a history. |
| R2 | The first package | done, 2026-11-15 | Part 1: `@mark1russell7/market` has the records of a product, an offer and a price reading, with their checks. It also has the GTIN check, the match keys, the cost of an order and the storage rules of a source. Part 2 (done, 2026-11-15): the site is a demo. It chooses the offer to buy from sample offers, with a chart of the unit price for each count. It also checks a GTIN and shows the storage rules of each source. Part 3 (done, 2026-11-15, 0.1.1): `offersOfPart` gives the offers of a part, by the brand and the part number of its maker, and `bestOffer` chooses the offer to buy. |
| R3 | The sources | in progress | Make `@mark1russell7/market-sources`: one adapter for each seller, the official API first. An adapter keeps no store: it gives the current offers and readings of one request. Part 1: the parsers of the answers of eBay, Best Buy and Digi-Key, and the terms of each source. Part 2: the clients of the APIs, with their tokens, on a transport port. The scheduler and the classifier of `polite-crawler` keep the pace and the stops of each host. The send, the token store and the reaction to an answer are in `polite-crawler/api` (0.6.1), and the redaction is `redactSecrets` of `@mark1russell7/text`. Part 3: the private package `@market/crawler` with the transport, the keys and the live read command, and a first live read with real keys. |
| R4 | The live crawler | in progress | A watch reads its sources on a schedule and keeps a board of current prices. Part 1 (done, 2026-11-15, 0.1.2): the board in `@mark1russell7/market`. `keepObservations` keeps the latest reading of each offer while the terms of its source permit it. `planReads` reads each entry again before its readings pass their limit. A source with `no-store`, as Digi-Key, is not on the board at any time: a program reads it for each request. Part 2 (done, 2026-11-15, 0.1.3): the watch command of `@market/crawler`, a service on the loops of `@mark1russell7/service`. It reads one entry at a time, the entry that is due for the longest time first, and writes the board after each read. Its tests use a fake clock and a fake transport. Part 3: a first live watch with real keys. |
| R5 | History and trends | later | The price history of each offer and its trend, with statistics from `@mark1russell7/rigor-statistics`. A page shows them. On 2026-11-12 the owner chose current prices only, with no source of a history. This item waits until a trend matters for a real purchase (refer to [`HAND-CRAWL.md`](HAND-CRAWL.md)). |
| R6 | The first release to npm | done, 2026-11-15 | `@mark1russell7/market` and `@mark1russell7/market-sources` got their build (`tsdown`), their `publishConfig`, their API reports and their licenses, and they lost `private`. The owner did the bootstrap of the two new packages on 2026-11-15. Both are on npm: `market` 0.1.3 and `market-sources` 0.2.2. After this release, a version change on `main` publishes a package. |
