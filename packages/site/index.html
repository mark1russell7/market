market

The records of a market, and the choice of an offer. Sellers write one part in many forms. The package finds the offers of the part, and it chooses the offer to buy, with a reason for each offer that it skips.

Choose the offer to buy

The page runs offersOfPart and bestOffer on 9 sample offers from 4 sources. The sources and the prices are not real.

Conditions that you accept

The unit price for each count

    The count goes from 1 to 120 units. A dashed line is an offer that is not in stock. The band under the chart shows the best offer at each count. Click the chart to choose a count.

        Check a GTIN

        A GTIN is the number of a barcode: a UPC-A, an EAN-13, a GTIN-8 or a GTIN-14. Its last digit is a check digit. The page runs parseGtin and matchKeys.

        How long a store keeps a reading

        The terms of each source limit how long a program keeps its prices. The page runs isKept, keepUntil, permitsHistory and permitsTrend on the sample terms.
